02 — Two briquettes

One smart combination.

Discover how GoldFlame can combine different briquette shapes for starting and sustaining the fire.

Step 1 · Start

Honeycomb Briquettes

Honeycomb briquettes can be used to help establish the initial fire, following the stove’s recommended lighting method. Their distinctive perforated design allows air to pass through the briquette.

Step 2 · Optional

Pillow Briquettes

Once the fire is established, pillow briquettes may optionally be added to help maintain the heat needed for cooking, depending on the stove design and cooking requirements.

Benefits of Charcoal Briquette Stoves & Cooking

Charcoal briquette stoves offer a practical and versatile cooking solution, combining the sustained heat of charcoal briquettes with the convenience of a purpose-built stove.

Key Benefits

  • Sustained Heat Performance – Enjoy extended cooking with fewer fuel replacements, depending on briquette and stove performance.
  • Efficient Fuel Utilisation – Make effective use of the heat generated by charcoal briquettes.
  • Versatile Cooking Applications – Ideal for grilling, roasting, boiling, simmering and everyday cooking.
  • Reduces reliance on LPG and other fuels – Variations in availability of LPG will not impact your cooking
  • Distinctive Smoky Flavour – Adds a traditional charcoal-grilled flavour to food.
  • Convenient Fuel Handling – Uniform briquettes are easy to load, store and handle.
  • Outdoor Cooking Convenience – A practical choice for outdoor kitchens, food stalls and recreational cooking.
  • Cost Affordability & Efficiency – Offers an opportunity to optimise cooking fuel costs through efficient stove and fuel use.
