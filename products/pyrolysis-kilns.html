Outputs

Products generated through our kilns

01

Lump Charcoal

Premium-quality charcoal with high fixed carbon content, long burn duration, and low ash generation.

02

Charcoal Briquettes

Charcoal is further processed into high-quality briquettes designed to meet diverse fuel requirements across the hospitality sector, industrial applications, and other commercial uses.

03

Biochar

Carbon-rich material engineered for agriculture, carbon sequestration, soil enhancement, and environmental applications.

04

Wood Vinegar

A valuable pyroligneous acid used in agriculture, composting, odor management, and soil health improvement.

05

Charcoal Tar

A recoverable pyrolysis by-product suitable for numerous applications and industrial and research applications.
