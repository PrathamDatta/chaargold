About CHAARGOLD & our products

What does CHAARGOLD specialise in?

CHAARGOLD is a charcoal and carbon solutions company specialising in premium charcoal products, charcoal briquettes, pyrolysis and retort kilns, charcoal manufacturing machinery, and turnkey carbon production solutions. We work across product manufacturing, equipment supply and customised project development to serve commercial, industrial and institutional requirements.

Where is CHAARGOLD located?

Our head office is in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and our manufacturing facility is located in Penukonda, Anantapur district, Andhra Pradesh. From our manufacturing base, we serve customers across South India and other parts of India and globally.

What types of charcoal products does CHAARGOLD manufacture?

CHAARGOLD offers different types of charcoal briquettes, including hexagonal, pillow and honeycomb briquettes. Our products are developed for applications requiring consistent heat, efficient combustion and convenient handling, subject to the intended application and product specification.

What are the different types of charcoal briquettes available?

The main types we work with include hexagonal charcoal briquettes, pillow charcoal briquettes and honeycomb charcoal briquettes. Each has a different shape and structure, making them suitable for different stove designs, cooking applications and commercial requirements.

Are CHAARGOLD charcoal briquettes available for wholesale and bulk orders?

Yes. We cater to bulk and commercial enquiries for charcoal briquettes. Businesses, distributors, retailers, restaurants and other institutional buyers can contact us to discuss product availability, packaging, quantities and supply requirements.

What is the difference between hexagonal, pillow and honeycomb charcoal briquettes?

Hexagonal briquettes typically feature a central hole that supports airflow during combustion. Pillow briquettes have a compact, rounded shape designed for convenient handling and use. Honeycomb briquettes feature multiple airflow openings that support combustion. The most suitable option depends on the stove, intended application and desired burning characteristics.

Does CHAARGOLD manufacture charcoal-making machines and briquette machinery?

CHAARGOLD offers charcoal production and briquette manufacturing equipment solutions, including machinery used for biomass processing, mixing, grinding, extrusion and briquette formation. Equipment configurations can be discussed based on the customer's production requirements and project scope.

Does CHAARGOLD supply complete charcoal manufacturing plants?

Yes. We can discuss complete charcoal manufacturing unit requirements, including production machinery, processing equipment, plant configuration, installation and commissioning support. Our approach is to understand the customer's feedstock, production objectives and available infrastructure before recommending a suitable setup.

Does CHAARGOLD manufacture pyrolysis kilns and retort kilns?

CHAARGOLD specialises in pyrolysis and retort kiln solutions for biomass carbonisation and charcoal production. Our equipment can be configured for different biomass feedstocks and project requirements. Biochar, kilns & wood vinegar

What is BIOCHAARGOLD?

BIOCHAARGOLD is the biochar-focused brand associated with CHAARGOLD, dedicated to biochar production solutions, biomass carbonisation technology, pyrolysis equipment and related carbon solutions. The brand focuses on supporting biochar production through suitable equipment, technical solutions and project-oriented services.

What is biochar and how is it produced?

Biochar is a carbon-rich material produced by heating biomass in a controlled, oxygen-limited environment through a process known as pyrolysis. Suitable feedstocks can include wood, agricultural residues, bamboo and other forms of biomass. The resulting biochar can be used in various applications, including soil improvement and agricultural systems, depending on its properties and quality.

What types of biomass can be converted into biochar?

A range of biomass materials can be used for biochar production, including wood chips, bamboo, coconut residues, Prosopis juliflora, agricultural residues and certain forestry wastes. The suitability of each feedstock depends on its moisture content, particle size, composition and the intended biochar application.

Does BIOCHAARGOLD manufacture biochar kilns?

BIOCHAARGOLD focuses on biochar production solutions through pyrolysis and retort kiln technology. Our equipment solutions are intended to support the conversion of suitable biomass materials into biochar through controlled carbonisation. Customers can contact us to discuss equipment configurations and project requirements.

What is a biochar pyrolysis kiln?

A biochar pyrolysis kiln is equipment designed to thermally process biomass under controlled, oxygen-limited conditions to produce biochar. Depending on its design, the system may also incorporate insulation, vapour condensation, gas handling and other supporting components. Kiln selection depends on feedstock, capacity and operating requirements.

What factors determine the capacity of a biochar production kiln?

The production capacity of a biochar kiln depends on several factors, including the type and quantity of biomass feedstock, its moisture content, particle size, kiln design and carbonisation cycle. Batch capacity and overall production output can also vary depending on operating conditions and processing time. At BIOCHAARGOLD, we consider these factors when discussing biochar pyrolysis kiln configurations and customised biomass carbonisation solutions for small-scale, commercial and industrial projects.

Does BIOCHAARGOLD provide complete biochar production plant solutions?

We can discuss complete biochar production plant requirements, from selecting a suitable pyrolysis or retort kiln to integrating supporting equipment and planning installation and commissioning.

Project configurations are considered based on feedstock availability, production capacity, site conditions and operational requirements.

What is wood vinegar and how is it obtained during pyrolysis?

Wood vinegar, also known as pyroligneous acid, is a liquid condensate obtained from the cooling of vapours released during biomass carbonisation. Suitable pyrolysis systems can incorporate condensation and collection equipment to recover this liquid by-product. Its composition and potential applications depend on the feedstock and production process.

Does BIOCHAARGOLD offer wood vinegar recovery systems?

Wood vinegar recovery can be considered as part of a suitable pyrolysis or retort kiln configuration. Depending on the project, the system may include vapour condensation and liquid collection components. Customers can discuss their recovery requirements with our team when enquiring about a biochar production system.

What are the applications of biochar?

Biochar is used in several areas, including soil amendment, compost enrichment, horticulture, agricultural applications and certain carbon removal projects. Its suitability depends on its physical and chemical properties, production method and intended use. Product testing and application-specific assessment are important when selecting biochar for a particular purpose.

Does BIOCHAARGOLD support commercial, agricultural and village-level biochar projects?

Yes. We are interested in supporting different scales of biochar production, from commercial and industrial installations to decentralised and village-level biomass carbonisation projects. Equipment requirements can be evaluated based on locally available feedstocks, expected production volumes, site conditions and project objectives. Organisations, agricultural groups, institutions and project developers can contact us to discuss potential solutions.
