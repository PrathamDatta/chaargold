About us

Who we are

ChaarGold is a vertically integrated manufacturing company specializing in the production and supply of charcoal briquettes, biochar, wood vinegar, smoke oil and related carbon-based products. We also offer turnkey pyrolysis plant solutions, providing end-to-end support from project planning and engineering design to equipment manufacturing, installation, commissioning, and operational training.

Our company was built on a simple but powerful idea: that the ancient art of charcoal making could be elevated by modern science, engineering rigor, and a deep commitment to sustainability. Today, ChaarGold stands as one of the most trusted names in carbon manufacturing, serving clients across hospitality, agriculture, textile, energy, chemical, and environmental sectors.

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka with an expertly engineered 5-acre manufacturing facility in Penukonda, Andhra Pradesh, we operate several production lines and a dedicated R&D division — all under one roof, all to one standard of excellence.

A family-run business

At ChaarGold, we are a family-run business built on unity, passion and a shared vision for a sustainable future. We believe in the strength of teamwork, the value of diverse perspectives, and the importance of balancing family values with professional excellence.

Our approach combines personal commitment with a strong sense of responsibility, driving us to build lasting partnerships, deliver dependable solutions, and grow with integrity. Together, we are committed to advancing sustainable biomass solutions through innovation, collaboration and purpose.

Founder & Chief Technology Officer

Prem Raj

Prem Raj is the Founder and Chief Technology Officer of CHAARGOLD, bringing over 15 years of experience in sustainable energy, biomass conversion, and carbon solutions. An engineer, innovator, patent holder, and passionate agriculturist, he combines technical expertise with practical industry knowledge to develop advanced pyrolysis technologies and high-performance carbon products. With global exposure across diverse markets, he is also a successful hotelier and brand-builder, driven by a vision to create sustainable businesses that deliver lasting value for customers, communities, and the environment.
